What Is Phishing?

Phishing is when scammers pretend to be someone you trust. They send fake messages or links to trick people into sharing passwords or private information.

Example of a Fake Message

Imagine getting a message saying your Instagram account will be deleted unless you click a link. This could be a phishing scam because the message tries to scare you into acting quickly.

Warning Signs

Be careful with messages that ask for your password, contain strange links, or tell you to act immediately. Scammers may also pretend to be a company you know.

What Should You Do?

Do not click suspicious links or share your password. Open the official social media app yourself to check your account. Report messages that look fake.
