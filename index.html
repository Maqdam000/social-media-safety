Welcome!

This website helps college students learn how to stay safe on Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat.

Why Is Social Media Safety Important?

Many college students use social media every day. Scammers may send fake messages to steal passwords and personal information. Learning about online safety can help students protect their accounts.

What Will You Learn?

You will learn how to recognize phishing messages, avoid suspicious links, create strong passwords, and use two-step verification.
