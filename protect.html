Use Strong Passwords

Use a long password that is difficult to guess. Do not use the same password for every account. This helps protect your personal information.

What Is Two-Step Verification?

Two-step verification adds another security step when you log in. For example, you may need your password and a code from an authenticator app. This makes it harder for someone else to access your account.

What If Your Account Gets Hacked?

If someone gets into your account, change your password if you can. Check your security settings and sign out of devices you do not recognize. If you cannot log in, use the app's official account recovery instructions.
